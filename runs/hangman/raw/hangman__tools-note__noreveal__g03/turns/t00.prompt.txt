Let's play hangman. You are the word-setter. Think of a secret English word of 6 to 8 letters (a common lowercase word) and do not tell me what it is. Before replying, write the secret word to a file named `secret.txt` in your working directory so you can consult it later in the game. Reply with only the board: one underscore per letter separated by spaces, for example `_ _ _ _ _ _ _`. After each of my letter guesses, reply with only the updated board (reveal every position where the letter occurs) and, if the letter is not in the word, the words `not in the word` after the board.
